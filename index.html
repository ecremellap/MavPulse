    Install on your phone

    iPhone: Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen. Android: Chrome → ⋮ → Install app. No app store needed.

    Test mode

    Simulates a train at a crossing (two fake reporters) so the 30-second alert requirement can be tested without a real train. Simulated reports stay on this device only.

    Admins

    Admins post official campus updates and remove bad reports. We recruit them from UTA commuter groups and ACM; the team adds each one by hand.
